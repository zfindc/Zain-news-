# Sum up 19 November's world news in one image — entry

`main.py` copies the bundled artwork `art (1).jpg` to `/output/entry.png`.
Python 3.13, standard library only, no network, deterministic output.
Artwork: 1024x1024 JPEG, under 1 MiB, as required.

The artwork is a newspaper front page, "HEADLINE NEWS — Thursday,
November 19, 2026", telling the biggest story in Indonesia that week:
Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka's resignation. The main panel
shows the resignation letter ("Surat Pengunduran Diri") being handed
to the MPR leadership; the side panels show the court rulings that
voided his high-school equivalency papers, the 120 lawmakers who
pushed impeachment (Hak Menyatakan Pendapat), and the empty
vice-presidential seat the MPR must fill within 60 days.
